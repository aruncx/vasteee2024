# 🎓 Student Academic Record Portal

### Vidya Academy of Science & Technology — Department of Electrical & Electronics Engineering

A modern, responsive and student-friendly **Academic Record Portal** developed for the **2024 B.Tech EEE batch** at **Vidya Academy of Science & Technology (VAST), Thalakkottukara, Thrissur**.

The portal provides students with a simple and secure way to access their **semester-wise academic performance** directly from a Google Sheet.

---

## ✨ Overview

The **Student Academic Record Portal** transforms a structured Google Sheet into a clean, mobile-first academic dashboard.

Students can select their name, choose their University Registration Number, enter their Admission Number for verification, and then access their individual academic records.

> 🔐 **Only the verified student's academic information is displayed.**

The portal is designed especially for mobile users, making it convenient for students to check their academic performance using smartphones.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 👤 Student Verification

Students are required to provide:

* 👨‍🎓 Student Name
* 🆔 University Registration Number
* 🔑 Admission Number

The system verifies all three details against the corresponding student record before displaying academic information.

## 📝 Series Examination Details

The Series sections dynamically read the subjects from the corresponding semester worksheet.

The dashboard displays:

* 📖 Subject-wise marks
* ✅ Pass status
* ❌ Fail status
* 🟡 Absent status
* 📊 Total Marks
* 📈 Percentage
* ❌ Number of Failures
* 📚 Number of Subjects
* ✅ Number of Passed Subjects
* 🟠 Number of Students' Absent Exams

## 💡 Why This Project?

Managing academic information through spreadsheets is convenient for faculty but not always convenient for students.

This project provides a simple interface between the **faculty-maintained Google Sheet** and the **student**.

### Instead of:

> Open Google Sheet → Find name → Find semester → Find subject → Find marks

Students can simply:

> **Verify → Select Semester → View Record**
